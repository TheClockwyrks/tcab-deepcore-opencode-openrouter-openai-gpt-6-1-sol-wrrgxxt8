# Deepcore

Descent is free. Coming home is expensive. Stranded on a dead mining world, you have a drill, a jetpack, and the shell of an escape rocket. Below your boots is enough mineral wealth to buy a way off the planet—if you can carry it back.

Cut your own shafts through four strata of alien rock. Follow glittering mineral seams, fill the cargo bay, and decide when to turn around. Slots are only half the problem: a heavy haul climbs slower, burns more fuel, and can leave your jetpack unable to lift. Sometimes the smartest thing to bring home is less.

The outpost turns each successful return into another chance. Sell your ore, patch the suit, replenish your fuel, and choose between a stronger drill, a better jetpack, a scanner, and another piece of the rocket. Save at the pad before taking your next risk. Standard gives you a checkpoint to return to; Hardcore makes every trip final.

Farther down, pale gas seeps breathe through stone and molten seams glow beneath your feet. Find the rare crystals your rocket needs, then make the deepest wager of all: take a Sample from the inexhaustible Core and race a ninety-second countdown back to the launch pad. Five components. One ignition. Home.
